Top Four Las Vegas Attactions
Attraction Description
Las Vegas Strip A 4.2-mile boulevard lined with famous mega-resorts, casinos, restaurants, and entertainment venues.
Fountains of Bellagio A free, iconic water show featuring thousands of nozzles and lights synchronized to music.
Fremont Street Experience A five-block pedestrian mall in downtown Las Vegas featuring a massive overhead LED canopy, concerts, and ziplining.
High Roller Observation Wheel A 550-foot-tall giant Ferris wheel at the LINQ Promenade that gives panoramic views of the city.
